This page is a snapshot of what I am focused on right now, a few weeks after wrapping up three years at smallcase.

### Exploring AI-native workflows

I am rebuilding my own product workflows around AI tools — research, synthesis, prototyping — to see which steps genuinely change and which just get renamed.

### Building internal tooling

I am building small tools for my own use, mostly to shorten the distance between a question and an answer. It keeps my hands close to how software actually gets made.

### Writing

I am writing up what I learned at smallcase, partly to document it and partly to test whether the lessons hold outside their original context.

### Learning

I am reading more deeply on how large language models work, enough to hold sharper opinions about what is worth building with them.
